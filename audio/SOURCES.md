Acceptable licences are ones that permit redistribution and modification:
CC0, CC-BY (with attribution kept here), or self-recorded material.
CC-BY-NC is **not** acceptable - Moleverse may end up in modpacks that are
monetised, and non-commercial clauses would break that.
